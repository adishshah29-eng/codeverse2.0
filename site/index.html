The Dossier

File 01 · The Institute

DJSCE

SVKM's Dwarkadas J. Sanghvi College of Engineering, established in 1994, is an autonomous engineering institution known for academic excellence, innovation and industry exposure. It offers undergraduate, postgraduate and Ph.D. programmes, its eligible undergraduate programmes are NBA accredited, and it holds an 'A' grade from NAAC.

Clubs like IEEE, ACM, DJS Unicode, DJS DJINIT.AI, DJS Compute, DJS Beats, DJS E-Cell and DJS Code AI give students room to build technical, creative, entrepreneurial and leadership skills.

File 02 · The Hosts

DJS Code AI

The official AI & ML club of DJSCE, built around one idea: learn by building, and build to create real impact. Bootcamps, technical events, hackathons, workshops and mentorship bring students closer to technology.

Members work on real-world projects, explore emerging tech, and take on outsourced projects for clients. A community where curiosity becomes capability.

File 03 · The Job

CodeVerse 2.0

A high-intensity heist where coding, strategy, investigation and decision-making collide. Hosted by DJS Code AI in collaboration with Unstop, with the Institution's Innovation Council, DJSCE.

Crack cryptographic clues, debug systems, investigate digital trails, work with data and machine learning, solve algorithmic challenges and navigate strategic trade-offs. Every challenge feeds the final extraction.

The Professor's Rules

  1. Rule 1Team size. Teams of 2–3 members. One team per participant.
  2. Rule 2Two phases. The Royal Mint and The Escape. Only shortlisted crews go on to Phase 2.
  3. Rule 3Time & submission. Complete every challenge within the given time.
  4. Rule 4Fair play. Work independently and use only permitted resources. Sharing answers or work is prohibited.
  5. Rule 5Scoring. Points for completed challenges. Ties may be broken on time or tie-breakers.
  6. Rule 6Tech issues. Report them immediately. The organisers decide on extra time.
  7. Rule 7Disqualification. Cheating, tampering, unauthorised access or unfair practices may lead to disqualification.
  8. Rule 8Final decision. The organisers may modify rules, timings or challenges. Their decision is final.
How do I register?

Registration runs on Unstop from 29 September to 6 October. Hit Join the crew, form your team of 2–3 there and you're in.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes. The registration fee is ₹149 per team.

How many crews can enter?

Forty-five. Once the seats are gone, the door closes.

Where does the heist happen?

AIML Department, Dwarkadas J. Sanghvi College of Engineering, Mumbai, on 9 October. Phase 1 starts at 10 AM.

What should I bring?

Your laptop, its charger and your college ID.

Can beginners join?

Yes. The event is open to everyone, whatever your college, branch or experience.
