The Briefing

It's been five months since he found you.

No names. No pasts. Just a knock on the door, and a man who called himself The Professor.

He trained you. No phones. No families. No room for error.

Today, the training ends.
Today, you go in.

The Royal Mint holds the one machine that prints currency that has never officially existed. Control it, and you decide what money even means.

"Once you're inside, the plan is only as good as the crew executing it."

You are not hackathon teams.
You are the crew.

Your objective

Get in. Take control of the mint. Print what you came for. Get out before the walls close in.

The Professor's Rules

  1. Rule 1Three to a crew. Nobody goes in alone, and nobody brings a fourth.
  2. Rule 2Forty-five crews get in. Registration runs 29 September to 6 October on Unstop.
  3. Rule 3No one gets hurt. Original work only. No plagiarism.
  4. Rule 4Bring your own tools. Laptop, laptop charger, college ID.
  5. Rule 5Open to everyone. Any college, any branch, any level.
How do I register?

Registration runs on Unstop from 29 September to 6 October. Hit Join the crew, form your team of three there and you're in.

How many crews can enter?

Forty-five. Once the seats are gone, the door closes.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes. The registration fee is ₹99.

Where does the heist happen?

Dwarkadas J. Sanghvi College of Engineering, Mumbai, on 9 October.

Can beginners join?

Yes. The event is open to everyone, whatever your college, branch or experience.
